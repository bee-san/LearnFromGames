Read any app,
not only games

LFG reads games by OCR. Browsers, chat, news, ebooks and web visual novels mostly draw real text, and Android will hand that text over exactly. This page shows how LFG picks a source, how a lookup starts in an ordinary app, and the order to build it in.

How a lookup starts

Games read themselves. Apps wait to be asked.

Invisible tap strips over a feed would steal its scrolling and taps. LFG already leaves an app alone while its status bar shows. Ordinary apps get a new on-demand mode as their default; games keep automatic reading.

READ_AUTO

Games

Reads when the status bar is hidden. Tap strips stay up. Unchanged.

New · default for appsREAD_ON_DEMAND

Ordinary apps

Nothing is drawn until a trigger. Then the next tap is a lookup, and strips go away after it or on Back.

OCR for any screen

The game pipeline, started by hand

Everything reuses ReadingPipeline, ScreenAI or meikiocr, and Blocks. What is new is how a reading starts and how the result is shown. Where the tree and OCR both cover a place, the tree's text wins; OCR fills in images, canvas text and video frames.

Read the whole screen once

One screenshot, read, grouped into lines, strips laid over it until the next tap or Back.

Manga, comics, images, photos of signs and menus, screenshots

Read a region

Drag a box; only that part is read. Faster, and it ignores what is around it.

One speech bubble, one subtitle line

Keep reading

SettleDetector limited to a chosen region, a reading per settled change, until turned off. Lines are stored as in a game.

Video subtitles, live streams, slideshows

Privacy

  • Password fields and anything marked sensitive are never read or stored.
  • Lines from apps can be private (chats, email). Keeping them is its own setting, off by default.
  • Lookups work with that setting off.

Limits

  • FLAG_SECURE apps (most paid video, some banking) give a black capture. Detect it and tell the user.
  • Some apps hide or flatten their text: custom drawing, Compose without semantics, Flutter in some modes. OCR is the fallback.
  • Full-screen OCR costs ~320 ms; a region is the better default for continuous reading.
Open questions

What the issue leaves to decide

  1. Tile, floating button, or both?The issue says “and/or”. The tile costs nothing on screen; the bubble is one tap closer but covers part of the app.
  2. Does continuous reading store lines when app storage is off?Step 5 says lines are stored as in a game; the privacy note says app lines are off by default. Subtitles are rarely private, so a per-mode exception may make sense.
  3. Leave apps out of reading time by default?Scrolling a feed is not reading a game. The issue asks for a way to exclude them, not which way it starts.
  4. Do the strips stay up after one lookup?“Until the next tap or Back” suits a single look. Reading a whole article may want them to stay until Back.
